Paste a YouTube link into the field at the top of the window and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or press Fetch. You can also press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on macOS) and paste the link into the command palette.

## Videos and Shorts

A video opens with its preview beside a download panel.

- **Video and audio** lists the qualities the video really has, up to 4K. Higher qualities are merged with FFmpeg.
- **Audio only** downloads the best audio track and converts it to MP3.

Pick a quality and press Download. Progress, speed and time left show while it runs, and the file lands in your download folder.

## Playlists

Open a playlist link to see every video in it. Search the list and tick the videos you want, then download them in one run. Private and deleted videos are marked so you can't pick them.

A video that belongs to a playlist offers to load the whole list.

## Channels

Open a channel link to browse its Videos, Shorts, Live and Playlists tabs. Streams that are live or still to come are marked, and can't be picked.

## Many links at once

The Batch download page takes many YouTube and Instagram links, one per line. Each one opens so you can pick from it, then it all downloads in one run.

## Pause and resume

Pause a download and continue it later. If the app closes in the middle of one, the next start offers to resume it, and yt-dlp picks up where it left off.

## Transcribe after download

Turn on **Transcribe after download** and every finished download is transcribed too. The text file is saved next to the video. [Transcribe](/docs/guides/transcribe) explains the models and languages.

## Download options

The YouTube and Instagram pages have a Download options panel. It changes the folder and the file names for this session only and leaves the saved defaults in [Settings](/docs/guides/settings) alone.
