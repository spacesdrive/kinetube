kinetube is a free, open-source desktop app. It downloads videos, Shorts, playlists, channels, posts and reels, and transcribes them with Whisper. There is no account, no subscription and no telemetry.

Many downloaders are websites full of ads, or apps that want an account and a monthly fee. Transcription services want your files on their servers first. kinetube does both jobs on your machine. Paste a link, pick what you want, and the file lands in your download folder. Turn on transcription and a text file lands next to it.

## The tools inside

The real work is done by open-source tools you may already trust. kinetube installs them for you and puts them behind one window.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) | Reads and downloads the media |
| [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org) | Merges video and audio, and converts to MP3 |
| [whisper.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp) | Turns speech into text |
| [instaloader](https://github.com/instaloader/instaloader) | Reads Instagram profiles |

## What you can do

- **YouTube.** Videos and Shorts up to 4K, only in the qualities each video really has. Any video can also be saved as an MP3.
- **Instagram.** Posts, reels and stories. Open a profile, load up to 500 posts and download the ones you pick.
- **Transcription.** Five Whisper models, from Tiny (about 75 MB) to Large (about 2.9 GB), and 12 languages.
- **Pause and resume.** Pause a download and continue it later. If the app closes in the middle of one, the next start offers to resume it.
- **Updates.** The app tells you when a new version of kinetube or yt-dlp is out and installs it when you choose.
