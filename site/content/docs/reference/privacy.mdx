- kinetube has no server of its own, no account and no telemetry.
- It contacts YouTube and Instagram (with their image servers for thumbnails), and the hosts it installs tools and models from: GitHub, the FFmpeg build hosts, Hugging Face, and PyPI through pip.
- Downloads, transcripts, settings and Instagram logins stay on your computer.
- The fonts ship inside the app, so no page loads anything from a font service.

## The local server

kinetube runs a small server on your computer that the window talks to. It listens on `127.0.0.1` only. It refuses requests from other web pages and other host names, so a website you visit can't use it, and other computers on your network can't reach it.

## This website

This site loads no analytics and sets no cookies. It remembers your theme choice in your browser, and nothing else.
